# Drill 01 — Pose playground

Goal: detailed, deliberate posing on a real rig. No physics yet — clean
bone control first.

## Setup
1. Open your model (see `terabox/Scarlett.md`) or the demo scene.
2. Pose Mode. Turn on X-Ray + bone names so you can see what you grab.

## Drills
1. **Line of action.** Pose the full body around one clear S- or C-curve.
   Screenshot front + side. If the silhouette doesn't read in pure black,
   the pose isn't done.
2. **Weight.** Shift the hips so the weight sits on ONE leg. Counter-rotate
   the shoulders. Feet flat, never below the ground plane.
3. **Hands.** Pose every finger — no mitten hands. Thumb opposition sells it.
4. **Facing.** The chest faces where the body is GOING. Check from the top
   view: no crab-walking.
5. **Hold check.** Step through your pose at 2x zoom on each limb,
   joint by joint — the same limb-by-limb inspection used for renders.

## Pass criteria
- Full arm reach visible in frame, hands never clipped by the viewport edge.
- Silhouette reads at thumbnail size.
- You can name what every limb is doing and why.
